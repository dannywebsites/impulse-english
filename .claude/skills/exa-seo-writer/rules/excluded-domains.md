# Excluded Domains

These domains are filtered out of SERP research because they either:
- Don't represent competitive SEO content
- Are user-generated and inconsistent
- Dominate SERPs but aren't realistic competitors

## Domain list

Skip all results from:

- reddit.com
- wikipedia.org
- quora.com
- pinterest.com
- youtube.com
- facebook.com
- twitter.com
- x.com
- instagram.com
- tiktok.com
- amazon.com
- ebay.com
- linkedin.com
- medium.com (unless it's a high-authority publication)
- substack.com (user-generated)
- github.com (code, not articles)

## URL pattern filters

Also skip any URL containing these patterns, regardless of domain:

- `/forum/`
- `/thread/`
- `/profile/`
- `/user/`
- `/community/`
- `/discussions/`
- `/comments/`
- `/tag/`
- `/category/`
- `/author/`

## When to override

Very occasionally, a reddit or YouTube result is actually the best possible reference (e.g., a keyword that's specifically about a Reddit community). In that case:

- Note it in `brief.md` as "excluded from research but reviewed manually"
- Summarize its key points for the brief
- Do NOT include it in the competitor word count stats

## Rationale

- **Reddit/Quora**: user-generated, no SEO structure to learn from
- **Wikipedia**: different ranking dynamics, not a realistic competitor
- **YouTube**: different format entirely
- **Amazon/eBay**: product listings, not articles
- **Social platforms**: not article SEO
- **Forums**: thread-based, not cohesive articles
