# Introduction Template

## Purpose
Hook the reader, establish relevance, and preview the value they'll get.

## Structure (150-200 words)

### Sentence 1: The Hook
Pick ONE of these formats:
- **Stat hook**: "Over 4.2 million people searched for [keyword] last month..."
- **Problem hook**: "Choosing the wrong [product] costs the average user $X per year."
- **Question hook**: "What if you could [desired outcome] in half the time?"
- **Bold claim**: "[Product/approach] isn't what most people think it is."

### Sentence 2-3: The Problem/Context
- State the specific pain point your reader has
- Use "you" language — speak directly to them
- Include the primary keyword naturally

### Sentence 4: The Quick Answer (for roundups/comparisons)
- Name your top pick immediately: "After testing 20+ options, [Product] stands out for..."
- For guides: Preview the outcome: "By the end of this guide, you'll know exactly how to..."

### Sentence 5-6: The Preview
- List 2-3 things the reader will learn
- Keep it specific, not vague

## Rules
- Primary keyword MUST appear in first 100 words
- Maximum 5 sentences per paragraph
- No "In this article, we will explore..."
- No "In today's digital landscape..."
- No "Whether you're a beginner or expert..."
- No passive voice in the hook
- Use contractions (you'll, it's, won't)

## Examples

### Good (Roundup):
"Most project management tools waste more time than they save. After spending 3 months testing 15 platforms with real teams, the differences are clear. Notion comes out on top for teams under 20 people — it's the only tool that handles docs, tasks, and wikis without feeling bloated. Here's how every option stacks up, who each one is actually built for, and which ones to avoid."

### Good (Guide):
"Dropshipping sounds simple until you actually try it. The failure rate sits above 90% in the first year, and most of those failures happen in the first 60 days. This guide covers the exact steps to launch a profitable store — from finding a niche that isn't saturated to setting up suppliers that won't ghost you after the first order."

### Bad:
"In today's fast-paced digital world, project management tools have become an essential part of every organization's toolkit. Whether you're a small startup or a large enterprise, finding the right solution can be a daunting task. In this comprehensive guide, we'll explore the top options available in the market."
