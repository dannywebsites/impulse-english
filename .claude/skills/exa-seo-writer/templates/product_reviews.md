# Product Reviews Template (Roundup / Alternatives / Review)

## Overview
Each product gets its own **H2** section (one level below the article's H1 title). The top pick gets the most detail.
Total products: **max 7** (cap is binding — engagement drops past 7). Quality over quantity.

## Section Structure Per Product

### Heading Format
`## #[Rank]. [Product Name] — Best for [Specific User Segment]`

Examples:
- `## #1. Notion — Best for Small Teams Under 20 People`
- `## #2. Monday.com — Best for Enterprise Workflow Automation`
- `## #3. Trello — Best for Simple Visual Task Tracking`

The segment must be SPECIFIC. Not "Best Overall" — that's lazy. WHO is it best for?

**Forbidden sub-headings:** never use "Key features" or "Key benefits" as a sub-heading. The word "key" is banned by `rules/humanizer.md`. Use **"What it does well"**, **"Main features"**, or **"Top features"** instead.

### Body Structure (200-400 words per product)

#### Opening (2-3 sentences)
- What the product IS in one sentence
- What makes it different from the others
- Include one specific metric if possible (users, uptime, speed, price)

#### Pros (3-5 bullets)
Every bullet must be:
- **Specific**: Not "easy to use" → "drag-and-drop task boards with 50+ ready-made templates"
- **Measurable where possible**: "99.9% uptime guarantee" not "reliable"
- **User-benefit focused**: Not features, but what the feature DOES for the user

#### Cons (2-3 bullets)
Must be HONEST and REAL:
- Not "could improve customer support" → "email-only support, no live chat, avg response 24hrs"
- Not "pricing could be better" → "jumps from $8/mo to $24/mo at the Pro tier with no middle option"
- Include at least one genuine dealbreaker for the wrong user

#### Key Differentiator (1-2 paragraphs)
- The ONE thing this product does better than every other option on the list
- Support with a specific example, number, or use case
- This is where you convince the right reader this is THEIR pick

#### Pricing
- List actual prices (tiers if applicable)
- Note free plan/trial if available
- Compare value: "At $10/mo, it's half the price of [Competitor] with similar features"

#### CTA
- `[Try Product Name Free](link)` or `[See Product Name Pricing](link)`
- Keep it one line, action-oriented

## Rules
- Product #1 (top pick) gets 350-400 words
- Products #2-3 get 250-350 words
- Products #4+ get 200-250 words
- Include at least one secondary keyword per product review
- Vary the structure slightly between products (don't make them all identical)
- Link to the product's main page, not deep inner pages
- Never fake pros or cons — readers and Google both detect BS

## Product Selection Order
1. **Top Pick** — best for the majority of readers
2. **Runner Up** — strong alternative with different strengths
3. **Budget Pick** — best value option
4. **Premium Pick** — best if money isn't the constraint
5. **Niche Picks** — best for specific use cases

## Featured Snippet List
Before the individual reviews, include a bullet list of all products:
```
## Best [Keyword]

1. **[Product A]** — Best for [segment]
2. **[Product B]** — Best for [segment]
3. **[Product C]** — Best for [segment]
```
This targets the featured snippet / position zero.
