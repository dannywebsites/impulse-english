## Why this set looks the way it does

The brief was "local intent". Measured, the geo-modified space does not contain 30 articles:

| keyword | vol | Sept | KD | main intent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| academia de ingles madrid | 1.300 | 2.900 | 32 | navigational |
| clases de ingles madrid | 480 | 880 | 34 | informational |
| academia de ingles en madrid | 170 | 480 | 42 | informational |
| mejor academia de ingles madrid | 40 | 70 | – | commercial |
| *the rest of 354 pulled keywords* | ≤30 | | | mostly navigational |

The top two are commercial head terms that belong to **service pages**, not the blog (already
ruled: Decisions Log, Decision 6, 2026-08-07). The tail is people hunting a named rival or a
street — `academia de ingles calle orense madrid`, `…chester…`, `…what's up…`, `…british council…`.

Widening the seeds only re-found the dictionary/worksheet trap: `verbos irregulares en ingles`
40.500 · `diccionario ingles` 33.100 · `hola en ingles` 22.200 · `traductor a ingles` 18.100 ·
`portadas de inglés` 18.100 (school notebook covers — **165.000 in September**). Owned by Google,
WordReference and DeepL, and confirmed as non-converting by the log's Decision 2.

**So "local" here means the audience and the proof, not the query string.** Every brief is a
question someone in the catchment asks at a decision moment, answered with local proof inside the
piece, linking into the local service pages.

## Interlinking (book §4)

Hub #1 is Tier 1. Every A/B piece links **up** to it in its intro. #28 is the conversion node and
is linked from most pieces. Each article links to at least two others (one up, one sibling), and
the cluster ring is circular. After publishing, link the relevant **service pages down** into the
cluster — the step that was skipped for the extranjero cluster and left 21 articles orphaned.
