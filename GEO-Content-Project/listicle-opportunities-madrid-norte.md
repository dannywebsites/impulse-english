Three findings that shape everything below.

**a) The "mejores academias de inglés en [barrio]" vein is nearly exhausted.**

The **"mejores…" phrasing itself has no volume at barrio level.** Per the Maverick broad-vs-narrow
rule (§2), target the **broad head term** (`academia de inglés en X`) as the page focus and drill
"las mejores / cuál elegir" into H2s. Do not make the zero-volume superlative the page target.

**c) The SERP gap is real and verified.** Live pull for `mejores academias de ingles alcobendas`
(2026-08-08): page 1 = 6 local-pack results, five directories (lomejordelbarrio, infoisinfo,
cronoshare, cylex, PáginasAmarillas) and academy homepages. **Not one academy has written a real
comparison article.** Maverick §2 winnability check — "if no competitor uses the keyword in their
title tag, it's ripe for the picking" — is satisfied.

Contrast with `mejores academias de ingles en madrid`, where page 1 is *already* four rival
academies self-ranking in their own listicles (British Academia #1 = itself; Oxinity #1 = itself;
American Language Academy praises itself). **Google tolerates the self-rank.** The tactic works —
it just has to be pointed at an unclaimed SERP.

- SERP: directory + local-pack shape, same as Alcobendas.

**2. Academias de inglés en Alcobendas — comparativa real**

### Tier 2 — local listicles with more volume than "academias"

**5. Los mejores intercambios de idiomas de Madrid (2026)** ← *best volume-to-difficulty ratio found*

- Content: EOI, CEPAs, Ayuntamiento, bibliotecas, intercambios. DEEPR = **Problem-solving**.
- Angle: genuinely useful, honest about where free stops working. Highest-trust piece on the list.

**7. Los mejores cursos intensivos de inglés en Madrid**
- Target: `cursos de ingles intensivos madrid` · **110/mo** · HIGH (92) · CPC 6,49 €

**9. Centros examinadores y preparadores Cambridge en Madrid**

## 3. ⚠️ Fix before writing anything new

`mejor-academia-ingles-chamartin.md` and `mejores-academias-ingles-chamartin.md` are
**near-duplicates** — identical `question`, identical `seoTitle` ("Mejores academias de inglés en
Chamartín"), identical `paaAnswer`, identical seven-item list. Two URLs, one intent.

Maverick §2 is explicit: *"One intent = ONE page… this doesn't mean you're writing two different
articles with the same keyword."* Against a 10/mo term at HIGH (93) competition, splitting the
signal is pure loss. **Consolidate to one, 301 the other.**

The Tetuán pair (`mejor-academia-ingles-tetuan.md` / `mejores-academias-ingles-tetuan.md`) is
milder — genuinely different angles (criteria guide vs. ranked comparison) — but still one keyword
family. Check GSC for which one earns impressions before deciding whether to merge.

## 4. Rules any of these must follow

- **Competitor neutrality** (`feedback_competitor_neutrality`): rivals get neutral verified facts
  only — no praise, no attack. Impulse always #1. No invented quotes; use the 183 real Google
  reviews (verbatim, never reworded — `project_reviews_pipeline`).
- **Writer**: `seo-blog-writer --format listicle` (never `geo-pages` for blog articles —
  `feedback_skill_by_page_type`). Listicle mode gates answer-first order, ItemList schema and the
  verbatim-review check.
- **Category registration**: a free-form frontmatter `category:` needs 4 registrations or it
  silently falls back to Cambridge B2 First — badge, hub link *and* GHL lead source
  (`project_extranjero_blog_cluster`).
- **Pin every slug** if these run as a parallel batch (`project_blog_batch_windows`).
- Log the chosen targets in `seo references and book/SEO-Decisions-Log.md` with the book principle
  cited, per root CLAUDE.md.
