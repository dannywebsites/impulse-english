## WHY THIS MATTERS FOR GEO

The "Answer Capsule" is the single most important text block on any page for AI citations.

**Research finding:** 72% of pages cited by ChatGPT have an answer capsule present - making it the most consistent predictor of AI citation.

AI systems extract the first 40-60 words to use in responses. This text block must:
- Directly answer a likely user question
- Be self-contained (makes sense without context)
- Include specific, verifiable details
- Read naturally when spoken aloud

---

## ANSWER CAPSULE REQUIREMENTS

### Format Specifications
- **Length: Exactly 40-60 words** (critical - AI systems extract this length)
- Must be a self-contained answer
- Must read naturally when spoken aloud
- Must include at least one specific number

### Required Elements (all must be present)
1. **Business/service identification** - Who provides this service
2. **Specific area served** - Neighborhoods, not just city
3. **Credibility signal** - Years, certifications, job count
4. **Key differentiator** - What makes you different
5. **Specific number** - Price, time, rating, years, or count
6. **Call to action** - What to do next
7. **Contact info** - Phone number

### The 40-60 Word Formula
```
[Question hook OR Direct statement]? [Business name] has [served/provided] [specific area] for [X years/since XXXX]. [Key differentiator or specialty]. [Specific metric or offer]. [CTA with phone number].
```

---

## THREE ANGLES TO GENERATE

Always generate 3 versions with different lead angles:

### Angle 1: Authority Lead
Opens with experience, credentials, or track record.
```
"[Business] has been [Area]'s trusted [service] for [X years]..."
```

### Angle 2: Speed/Availability Lead
Opens with response time or availability promise.
```
"Need [service] in [Area]? [Business] responds in [X minutes]..."
```

### Angle 3: Value Lead
Opens with pricing or guarantee.
```
"[Service] in [Area] starting at [$X]. [Business] offers..."
```

---

## OUTPUT FORMAT

For each of the 3 versions, provide:
- The complete answer capsule text
- Word count (must be 40-60)
- Which angle it uses

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

**Too Generic:**
"We provide quality plumbing services to Boston and surrounding areas. Our experienced team is here to help with all your plumbing needs. Contact us today!"
- No specific numbers
- No neighborhood specificity
- Could be used for any location
- No personality

**Too Long:**
Going over 60 words dilutes the impact and AI systems may not extract it properly.

**Missing CTA:**
Informative but doesn't tell the reader what to do next.

**Missing Number:**
No years, price, time, or metric. Numbers create specificity and credibility.
