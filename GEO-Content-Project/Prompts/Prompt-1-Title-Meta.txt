---

## WHY THIS MATTERS FOR GEO

Title tags and meta descriptions are the FIRST thing AI systems evaluate when determining if a page is unique or a duplicate. Microsoft's research shows that LLMs group pages with similar titles together and only cite ONE.

**The #1 pattern that gets pages marked as duplicates:**
`[Service] in [City] | [Brand]`

This pattern is death for GEO. If you have 10 pages with "Plumbing Services in [City] | ABC Plumbing" - AI will treat them as duplicates.

---

## TITLE TAG REQUIREMENTS

### Format Specifications
- Length: 50-60 characters (optimal for display)
- Must be 100% unique per location
- Must be impossible to use for a different location

### Required Elements
1. **Lead with differentiator or specialty** - NOT the generic service name
2. **Include specific neighborhood/area** - NOT just the city
3. **Include a unique angle** (building type, specialty, response time, etc.)
4. **Brand name** - at the end, not the beginning

### Structure Options (choose one)
```
[Specialty/Angle] in [Neighborhood] | [Response/Promise] | [Brand]
[Building Type] [Service] in [Neighborhood] | [Brand]
[Urgency Word] [Service] in [Specific Area] | [Differentiator] | [Brand]
```

### FORBIDDEN Patterns (Never Use)
- "[Service] in [City] | [Brand]"
- "[City] [Service] | Professional [Service]"
- "[Brand] - Your Local [City] [Service Provider]"
- "Best [Service] in [City] | [Brand]"
- "[Service] Services in [City] Area"

---

## META DESCRIPTION REQUIREMENTS

### Format Specifications
- **MAXIMUM LENGTH: 160 characters** (NEVER exceed this limit)
- Optimal range: 150-160 characters
- Must include at least ONE specific number
- Must include specific area served
- Must include clear call-to-action
- Should end with phone number

### Required Elements
1. **Specific number** (years experience, response time, price, review count)
2. **Area-specific reference** (neighborhood or street names)
3. **Value proposition** (what makes you different)
4. **Call-to-action** (Call, Book, Schedule, Get Quote)
5. **Phone number** (at the end)

### Structure Template
```
[Area]'s trusted [service] for [X years]. [Specialty/differentiator]. [Specific offer with number]. [CTA]: [Phone]
```

## OUTPUT FORMAT

Generate 3 title tag options and 2 meta description options.

For each, include:
- The text
- Character count
- Which angle it uses
- Why it's GEO-optimized (brief note)

### Example Output Format:

**TITLE TAG OPTIONS:**

**Option 1: Specialty Lead**
`Emergency Plumber in Boston's Back Bay | 24/7 Brownstone Specialists`
- Characters: 62
- Angle: Specialty (brownstone expertise) + Urgency (emergency/24/7)
- GEO Note: Neighborhood-specific, building type reference - impossible to template

**Option 2: Response Time Lead**
`45-Min Response Plumber in Back Bay | Historic Home Experts | ABC`
- Characters: 58
- Angle: Speed promise + Specialty
- GEO Note: Specific metric + neighborhood + specialty creates uniqueness

**Option 3: Building Type Lead**
`Back Bay Brownstone Plumbing | 15-Year Local Expert | ABC Plumbing`
- Characters: 61
- Angle: Hyper-local building expertise
- GEO Note: Building type + years + "local" signals authentic presence

**META DESCRIPTION OPTIONS:**

**Option 1:**
`Back Bay's trusted emergency plumber for 15+ years. Brownstone pipe specialists serving Beacon Hill to South End. $89 service call, 45-min response. Call: (617) 555-0123`
- Characters: 158
- Numbers: 15+ years, $89, 45-min
- GEO Note: Multiple neighborhoods, specific pricing, response time

**Option 2:**
`Need a plumber in Back Bay? Mike's team has fixed 2,000+ brownstone plumbing issues since 2009. Same-day service, upfront pricing. Book now: (617) 555-0123`
- Characters: 156
- Numbers: 2,000+, 2009
- GEO Note: Named team member, specific job count, founding year
