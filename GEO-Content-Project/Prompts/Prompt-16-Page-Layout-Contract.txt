# PROMPT 16: PAGE LAYOUT CONTRACT (Impulse English / barrio pages)
## Presentation rules. The other prompts say WHAT to write; this says HOW it sits on the page.

---

## WHY THIS FILE EXISTS

Prompts 1-10 specify word counts, required facts and tone. None of them say anything about
layout. On the first rebuilt page (La Ventilla) that gap produced factually correct content
in three dense paragraphs that nobody would read. Danny flagged it on review.

Fifteen barrio pages are queued. A layout decided ad hoc gets repeated fifteen times, so it
is specified here once.

Reference implementation: `pages/ubicaciones/LaVentillaPage.tsx` (live 2026-08-02).

---

## RULE 0: THE DESIGN REFERENCE IS `/prueba-de-nivel-ingles/`

Danny's chosen reference. Every barrio page uses its tokens. Do not invent new ones:
small-caps labels, `<hr>`, dot lists and `font-light text-2xl` were tried on La Ventilla
and read as foreign, because nothing else on the site uses them.

**Section header block** (this exact three-part stack, every section):
```jsx
<div className="max-w-2xl mb-10">
  <span className="eyebrow mb-4">[ETIQUETA CORTA]</span>
  <h2 className="t-h2 text-zinc-900 mb-5">[TITULAR]</h2>
  <div className="rule"></div>
</div>
```

**The type scale** (defined in `src/index.css`, never hand-rolled):
| Token | What it is |
|---|---|
| `t-display` | Playfair Display, clamp 2.5-4.75rem, -0.03em. H1 only. |
| `t-h2` | Playfair Display, clamp 1.625-2.5rem, -0.02em, 600. |
| `t-h3` | Playfair Display, clamp 1.25-1.625rem, -0.01em, 600. |
| `t-lede` | clamp 1.06-1.25rem, 1.65 line-height. Lead paragraphs. |
| `t-small` | Footnotes, card bodies. |
| `eyebrow` | brand-red, uppercase, 0.18em tracking, 12px semibold. |
| `eyebrow-light` | Same on dark surfaces. |
| `rule` | `h-px w-16 bg-accent-blue/25`. Sits under every h2. |

**Colours** (`tailwind.config.ts`): `accent-blue` #12477d, `brand-red` #ea4e59,
`zinc-surface` #F4F4F5. Body copy `text-zinc-600`/`700`, headings `text-zinc-900`,
footnotes `text-zinc-500`.

**Fonts:** Playfair Display for headings via `font-serif` and the `t-*` tokens.
Inter for everything else (`font-sans`, `font-display`). Never repoint `font-display`
at Playfair: it is applied to ~355 elements and would reflow the whole site.

**Cards:** `card p-7` (hairline border + brand-tinted `shadow-card`), `card-interactive`
for hover lift, `card-quiet` on `zinc-surface`, `card-inverse` on dark. Never a flat grey
`shadow-md`, which is the loudest generic-template tell.

**Numbered steps** (reuse verbatim for routes and processes):
```jsx
<div className="card p-7">
  <span className="inline-flex w-9 h-9 rounded-full bg-brand-red text-white
                   items-center justify-center font-display font-bold mb-5">{n}</span>
  <h3 className="t-h3 text-zinc-900 mb-2">{title}</h3>
  <p className="t-small text-zinc-600">{body}</p>
</div>
```

**NO SCROLL ANIMATION.** `/prueba-de-nivel-ingles/` contains zero `reveal-on-scroll`.
`/sobre-nosotros/` animates nearly every block and Danny explicitly rejected that feel.
Barrio pages carry none.

## RULE 1: THE ANSWER CAPSULE STAYS A PLAIN PARAGRAPH

**Non-negotiable.** The 40-60 word answer capsule is rendered as a single `<p>`.

- NOT inside a card, callout, box, list, table, accordion or `<blockquote>`.
- NOT split across two paragraphs.
- NO icon, badge or coloured background attached to it.

Why: it is the block generative engines extract, Prompt-2 requires it to read naturally when
spoken aloud, and wrapping it in decorative markup risks it not being crawled as prose.

Styling that IS allowed, because it changes weight not structure:
- Larger type (`text-xl md:text-2xl`), light weight, relaxed leading.
- ONE `<strong>` around the single most citable fact (the distance, the price).
- The phone number as an `<a href={NAP.phoneTel}>` with `whitespace-nowrap`.

```jsx
<p className="text-xl md:text-2xl text-zinc-800 leading-relaxed font-light">
  ¿Buscas academia de inglés cerca de [BARRIO]? [Business] está{' '}
  <strong className="font-semibold text-zinc-900">[LA CIFRA MÁS CITABLE]</strong>: [detalle].
  [Credencial] desde [año], [diferenciador] y clases desde [precio].
  Reserva tu prueba de nivel gratuita con JP:{' '}
  <a href={NAP.phoneTel} className="text-accent-blue font-semibold hover:underline whitespace-nowrap">604 910 611</a>.
</p>
```

---

## RULE 2: EVERYTHING AFTER THE CAPSULE BREAKS INTO STRUCTURE

Prose after the capsule is capped at **three sentences per block**. Beyond that it becomes a
list, a grid or a table.

Standard shape: `<hr />`, then a two-column grid.

**Left column - the route.** Transport as a stop-by-stop list, not a sentence. Mark the stop
where the reader gets off. A filled dot for the relevant stops, grey for the rest.

**Right column - who comes from here.** Named colegios / IES as a checked list, then one
short paragraph on opening hours framed around fitting after school or work.

Both columns end with at most three sentences of supporting prose. Facts identical to the
prose version; only the shape changes.

## RULE 3: EVERY PAGE ENDS WITH THE CONVERSION BLOCK

Copied from the Barrio del Pilar pattern. Two columns, steps left, form right.

```jsx
<section className="section-lead px-6 surface-alt">
  <div className="container mx-auto max-w-6xl">
    <div className="grid grid-cols-1 lg:grid-cols-2 gap-12 items-center">
      <div>
        <h2 className="t-h2 text-zinc-900 mb-6">Pide Tu Prueba de Nivel</h2>
        {/* 3 numbered steps: contacto -> prueba de nivel 25 min con JP -> empieza */}
        <p className="text-zinc-900 font-bold text-lg mb-4">[PROXIMIDAD ESPECÍFICA]. Sin excusas.</p>
        <p className="text-zinc-600">{NAP.fullAddress}</p>
      </div>
      <div>
        <LeadForm
          title="Reserva Tu Prueba Gratuita"
          subtitle="Te contactamos en menos de 24h"
          ctaText="Reservar Ahora"
          source="[BARRIO-SLUG]"
          showPhone={true} showAge={true} showLevel={true}
          variant="refresh"
        />
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
```

### The one line that varies per barrio
`[PROXIMIDAD ESPECÍFICA]` must be the page's own distance fact, never a generic line:
- La Ventilla: "A una parada de metro desde Ventilla"
- Barrio del Pilar: "Academia a 500 metros de Metro Barrio del Pilar"
- Montecarmelo: "A 8 minutos en la L9 desde Montecarmelo"

### `source` is mandatory and barrio-specific
`source="la-ventilla"`, not `"barrio"` or the default. A generic source loses barrio
attribution in GHL, which is the only way to tell which pages convert.

⚠️ **Changing LeadForm props is a tracking-surface change.** Run `npm run verify:tracking`
and require ALL PASS before shipping. This applies to every barrio page, not just the ones
that touch analytics directly.

---

## RULE 4: NO EM DASHES OR EN DASHES

No U+2014, no U+2013. Anywhere: body copy, headings, FAQ answers, alt text, code comments.
Use a comma, a colon, a full stop, or restructure.

Standing constraint, decision 2026-05-29. It was already on the books when the first La
Ventilla build shipped with five of them, so check mechanically rather than by memory:

```bash
grep -n $'[—–]' pages/ubicaciones/[Page].tsx
```

Arrows (→) and middots (·) are fine. Hyphens in compounds are fine.

---

## RULE 5: IMAGE ORIENTATION MUST MATCH ITS CONTAINER

18 of the 21 photos in `new fotos/` are portrait (3024x4032). Barrio pages use a full-bleed
landscape hero.

- Heroes come ONLY from landscape or square sources.
- Portraits go in portrait or square containers (`aspect-[3/4]`, side-by-side splits, 3-up
  grids). Never a full-width band: `object-cover` crops ~60% out and reads as "zoomed",
  `object-fill` distorts faces.
- Set `object-position` deliberately where faces sit near the frame edge.

**Weight budget.** The hero is the LCP element and the site's 0.81s LCP is its single
clearest advantage over Cambridge House (5.58s). Keep hero images near the existing ~70 KB.
A 173 KB replacement was rejected on the La Ventilla build for exactly this reason: a better
photo is not worth losing the one board we win outright.
