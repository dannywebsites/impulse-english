# PROMPT 4: TEAM MEMBER BIO GENERATOR
## Reference File for GEO Content Project

---

## WHEN TO USE THIS FILE
Use this file when the user requests:
- Team bio
- Staff profile
- About the technician
- Meet the team
- Expert profile
- Technician bio
- About [person name]

---

## WHY THIS MATTERS FOR GEO

Named individuals with verifiable credentials are powerful E-E-A-T signals that AI systems actively look for. A page with "Our experienced technicians" is weaker than "Mike Torres, a 15-year licensed plumber who lives in Somerville."

**Team bios serve multiple GEO purposes:**
1. **E-E-A-T signal:** Real person with real credentials
2. **Local proof:** Team member who lives/works in the area
3. **Unique content:** Different team members per location = unique pages
4. **Trust builder:** Personal details humanize the business

---

## TEAM BIO REQUIREMENTS

### Format Specifications
- **Length:** 100-150 words
- **Perspective:** Generate BOTH third-person and first-person versions
- **Required elements:** See checklist below

### Required Elements
1. **Full name** (not "John D." or initials)
2. **Specific role/title**
3. **Years of experience** (total and/or in this area)
4. **Verifiable credentials** (licenses, certifications)
5. **Local connection** (lives in area, serves specific neighborhoods)
6. **Specific numbers** (jobs completed, years, certifications)
7. **Personal humanizing detail** (hobby, family, why they do this work)
8. **What makes them skilled** (specific expertise or approach)

---

## CONTENT STRUCTURE

### Third-Person Bio Structure:
```
[Name] has been [Area]'s go-to [role] for [X years]. [Credential/license]. [Specific job count or experience in area]. [Local connection - where they live/work]. [Personal detail that humanizes]. [What makes them particularly skilled]. When you call [Business], [Name] personally ensures [promise/standard].
```

### First-Person Quote Structure:
```
"I've been [doing this work] in [Area] for [X years], and [why this area is special to me]. [Personal connection to community]. [What I love about this work]. [Promise to customers]." — [Name], [Title]
```

---

## OUTPUT FORMAT

Generate both versions for each team member:

### Example Output:

---

**TEAM MEMBER BIO: Mike Torres**

**Third-Person Version** (127 words)

Mike Torres has been Back Bay's go-to emergency plumber for 15 years. A Massachusetts-licensed master plumber (License #PL-12345) with additional certification in historic building systems, Mike has personally completed over 2,000 plumbing repairs in brownstones from Beacon Hill to the South End.

A Somerville resident, Mike knows Boston's historic neighborhoods like the back of his hand—including the quirks of century-old plumbing that trip up less experienced technicians. When he's not under a sink, you'll find him coaching his daughter's soccer team in Davis Square.

What sets Mike apart is his diagnostic approach: he doesn't just fix the immediate problem, he identifies why it happened and how to prevent it. When you call ABC Plumbing for Back Bay service, Mike personally oversees every job.

---

My promise is simple: I'll treat your home like I treat my neighbor's. No shortcuts, no surprises, just honest work from someone who'll still be here when you need me again."

— Mike Torres, Lead Plumber & Back Bay Service Manager

## WHAT TO AVOID

**Generic Descriptions:**
- "Our experienced technicians are trained professionals..."
- "John has many years of experience in the industry..."
- "Our team is dedicated to customer satisfaction..."

**Missing Specificity:**
- "Licensed plumber" → "Massachusetts-licensed master plumber (License #PL-12345)"
- "Years of experience" → "15 years, 2,000+ jobs in Back Bay"
- "Serves the Boston area" → "Covers Back Bay, Beacon Hill, and South End; lives in Somerville"

**No Personal Touch:**
Bios without personal details feel corporate and templated. Add something human.

---

## ASSIGNING TEAM MEMBERS TO LOCATIONS

For multi-location businesses:
- Assign different team members to different location pages
- This creates naturally unique content per page
- If one person serves multiple areas, customize the bio for each:
  - Back Bay page emphasizes brownstone expertise
  - Cambridge page emphasizes triple-decker experience
  - Same person, different local focus
