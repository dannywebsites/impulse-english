## WHY THIS MATTERS FOR GEO

FAQPage schema has the HIGHEST citation probability of all schema types for AI search.

**Research findings:**
- Pages with FAQPage markup are 3.2x more likely to appear in Google AI Overviews
- FAQ content directly matches how people query AI systems ("How much does X cost in Y?")
- AI systems extract Q&A pairs as structured answers

**The problem:** Most FAQ sections are generic and identical across location pages.

**The solution:** Every FAQ must be location-specific, with questions and answers that could ONLY apply to that area.

---

## FAQ REQUIREMENTS

### Format Specifications
- **Number of FAQs:** 5-7 per page (optimal for schema and readability)
- **Answer length:** 40-60 words each (answer capsule format)
- **Question format:** Natural language, how a LOCAL person would ask
- **Answer format:** Direct answer first, then supporting detail

### Required FAQ Topics
Generate at least one FAQ for each of these topics:

1. **Pricing** - "How much does [service] cost in [area]?"
2. **Availability/Coverage** - "Do you serve [specific neighborhood]?"
3. **Response Time** - "How quickly can you get to [area]?"
4. **Local Expertise** - About local building types or conditions
5. **Emergency/Urgent** - After-hours or urgent service
6. **Process** - What to expect when booking
7. **Comparison** - Why choose you over competitors

### Answer Requirements
Each answer must include:
- Direct answer in first sentence
- At least one specific number
- Reference to the specific area
- Supporting detail that proves expertise

---

## FAQ STRUCTURE

### Question Format
Questions should sound like real people asking:
- ✓ "How much does emergency plumbing cost in Back Bay?"
- ✓ "Can you fix pipes in older brownstones in Beacon Hill?"
- ✗ "What are your service offerings?" (too corporate)
- ✗ "How do I contact you?" (not a real FAQ)

### Answer Structure
```
[Direct answer]. [Specific detail with number]. [Local context]. [Call to action if relevant].
```

### Example:
**Q: How quickly can a plumber get to Back Bay?**

A: We guarantee 45-minute response times to Back Bay emergencies. Our technician Mike Torres is based in nearby Somerville and knows the fastest routes to Commonwealth Ave, Marlborough Street, and Beacon Hill. For non-emergencies, we offer same-day appointments—call (617) 555-0123 to schedule.

## WHAT TO AVOID

**Generic Questions:**
- "What services do you offer?" → "What plumbing services do you provide in Back Bay?"
- "How do I contact you?" → "How do I schedule emergency plumbing in Back Bay?"
- "What are your hours?" → "Are you available for weekend emergencies in Back Bay?"

**Generic Answers:**
- "We offer competitive pricing" → "Service calls start at $89, repairs typically $150-$450"
- "We respond quickly" → "45-minute guaranteed response to Back Bay"
- "We serve the Boston area" → "We specialize in Back Bay, Beacon Hill, and South End brownstones"

**Corporate Tone:**
- "Thank you for your inquiry" → Just answer the question
- "We pride ourselves on..." → Show with specifics instead
- "Our team of professionals" → Name the actual person

## LOCATION-SPECIFIC FAQ IDEAS

### Questions That Force Local Specificity:
- "Can you work on [local building type] in [neighborhood]?"
- "How long does it take to get to [neighborhood] from your location?"
- "Do you know the permit requirements for [service] in [city]?"
- "Have you worked on buildings on [specific street]?"
- "What's different about [service] in [older/historic/etc.] [area] homes?"

These questions CAN'T be answered with generic responses—they require local knowledge.
