## FIRST-TIME BUYER PROGRAMS REQUIREMENTS

### Format Specifications
- **MAXIMUM LENGTH: 130 words** (strict limit)
- Must reference specific state/local programs by name
- Must include eligibility criteria or limits
- Must include specific dollar amounts or percentages
- Must cite official source with hyperlink

### Required Elements
1. **Program name(s)** - Specific state or local programs

**First-Time Buyer Programs in Texas**

Texas offers several first-time buyer programs through the Texas State Affordable Housing Corporation (TSAHC). The Homes for Texas Heroes program provides 5% of the loan amount for down payment and closing costs—a forgivable grant requiring no repayment if you stay 3 years. Income limits reach $153,300 for a family of 3+ in Travis County. The Home Sweet Texas program offers similar benefits with slightly different eligibility. As a TSAHC-approved lender, we process these applications in-house and can combine them with FHA or conventional loans.

Source: [Texas State Affordable Housing Corporation](https://www.tsahc.org/)

**First-Time Buyer Programs in California**

CalHFA's MyHome Assistance Program provides a deferred-payment junior loan up to 3.5% of the purchase price for down payment and closing costs. For buyers in high-cost areas, the Forgivable Equity Builder Loan adds up to $30,000 in additional assistance—forgiven after 5 years of ownership. Income limits vary by county; Los Angeles County caps at $180,000 for moderate-income buyers. CalHFA requires homebuyer education and works only through approved lenders. We're CalHFA-approved and have helped 200+ California first-time buyers access these programs since 2019.

Source: [California Housing Finance Agency](https://www.calhfa.ca.gov/)

## SOURCE CITATIONS (MANDATORY)

**You MUST include source URLs with every first-time buyer programs section.**

This is critical for:
1. **E-E-A-T signals** - Citing official HFA sources builds trust
2. **Backlink opportunities** - Links to state housing agencies add authority
3. **Verifiability** - Program details can be confirmed
4. **Legal protection** - Sourced financial program info is defensible

### Required Format:
```
**Source:** [State Housing Finance Agency](URL)
```

### Example:
```
**Source:** [Texas State Affordable Housing Corporation](https://www.tsahc.org/)
```

**Only use official sources:**
- State Housing Finance Agency (HFA) websites
- HUD state resources (hud.gov)
- City/county housing authority websites
- Official program portals

**If program details cannot be verified:** Tell the user "I couldn't verify current program limits for [state/program]. These change annually—please confirm with [HFA name] or I'll mark it as [NEEDS VERIFICATION]."

**CRITICAL:** First-time buyer programs change frequently. Always note the data date and recommend verification.

---

## WHAT TO AVOID

**Outdated information (CRITICAL):**
- Program amounts and limits change yearly
- Always verify current year limits
- Note if programs have funding caps or waitlists

**Generic statements (NEVER USE):**
- "Many programs are available" → Name specific programs
- "Income limits vary" → Give specific limit for target area
- "Ask your lender" → Position yourself as the expert

**Missing eligibility details:**
- First-time buyer definitions vary (usually 3 years)
- Some programs have homebuyer education requirements
- Some programs are forgivable vs. deferred loans

## COMMON PROGRAM TYPES

### State HFA Programs
- Down payment assistance (DPA)
- Closing cost assistance
- Below-market interest rates
- Mortgage credit certificates (MCC)

### Federal Programs
- FHA loans (3.5% down)
- VA loans (0% down for veterans)
- USDA loans (0% down for rural areas)
- Good Neighbor Next Door (HUD)

### Local Programs
- City housing authority grants
- County SHIP programs (Florida)
- Employer-assisted housing
- Community land trusts

### Special Population Programs
- Heroes/essential worker programs
- Teacher Next Door
- Veteran-specific programs
- Native American programs

---

## STACKING PROGRAMS

Many buyers can combine multiple programs:

**Example stack:**
1. FHA loan (3.5% down required)
2. State DPA (covers the 3.5%)
3. Local grant (covers closing costs)
4. MCC (ongoing tax credit)

**Key note:** Mention if you help buyers identify and stack eligible programs—this is a major value-add.

---

## ANNUAL UPDATES REMINDER

First-time buyer programs change frequently:
- Income limits update annually (usually April/May)
- Funding can run out mid-year
- New programs launch regularly
- Old programs sunset

**Best practice:** Include a note like "Limits current as of 2025" and recommend verifying current availability.
