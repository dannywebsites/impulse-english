## Read this before commissioning anything

### 1. Every competitor publishes prices. Impulse publishes none.

From the live SERPs, on page one, in the snippet:

| Competitor | What they show |
|---|---|
| CLS Idiomas | Año escolar Irlanda **desde 18.600 €** · boarding **desde 32.000 €** |
| infoidiomas | Año escolar Irlanda **desde 6.750 €** |
| Top School | Canadá trimestre **desde 9.704 €** · semestre **desde 10.987 €** |
| Google's AI Overview (Canadá) | *"desde los 17.000 € para colegios públicos con familia anfitriona"* |

`año escolar en irlanda precios` is **90/mo** and `Año escolar en Canadá precios`
is the **first related search** on that SERP. Enquiry-only is an approved decision and
this brief does not argue with it — but it is currently costing the section every
price-intent search, and the competitor range is so wide (6.750 € to 32.000 €) that a
family genuinely cannot calibrate. **The strongest available play is a "what actually
drives the price" article that gives the ranges and the variables honestly without
quoting an Impulse figure.** That needs Danny's sign-off, because it means publishing
market ranges. It is the single highest-value decision in this document.

### 2. "Trabajar en Irlanda" — I am revising my own recommendation downward.

I flagged this earlier as a new pillar on the strength of **1.900/mo at competition 29**.
Having pulled the actual SERP, that was too optimistic. Page one is:

> mites.gob.es (Spanish Ministry of Labour) · SEPE · InfoJobs · Glassdoor · StudentJob ·
> Club RCI · Dingoos · Blaveling

Government portals and job boards. An English academy does not outrank the Ministry of
Labour for "trabajar en Irlanda", and the **CPC of €1,23 against €12,90 for `año escolar
en canadá`** is the ad market telling you the commercial intent is a tenth as valuable.

**What is winnable is the English-gap long tail**, where Impulse is the actual answer and
none of those sites are:

- `Trabajo en Irlanda para españoles sin inglés` — a related search on that SERP, and
  the exact pain Impulse solves
- `¿Qué nivel de inglés necesito para trabajar en Irlanda?`
- `Trabajar en Irlanda verano` / `en verano con alojamiento incluido`

Recommendation: **no `/trabajar-en-irlanda/` pillar yet.** Write briefs 24–27 as blog
articles under the existing pillar, see whether they earn impressions in Search Console,
and only then decide. Building a pillar for a term you cannot rank is how you get a thin
page that competes with your own money pages.

### 3. AI Overviews sit above the fold on all three money terms.

`año escolar en irlanda`, `año escolar en canadá` and `trabajar en irlanda` all render an
AI Overview at rank 1. Every article needs a extractable answer in the first 80 words —
a direct, quotable sentence that answers the title question before any preamble. This is
also why the FAQ blocks are now open by default.

### 4. Confirmed dead — do not commission.

`mini estancia irlanda` returns **no data**, matching the earlier finding that
`mini estancias` = 0 and `viaje de estudios` = 0. The *product* is real; the phrase is
not searched. Cover it under `campamento de verano en irlanda` (140/mo).

### A · Año escolar en Irlanda — the money cluster
Links to `/ingles-en-el-extranjero/irlanda/ano-escolar/`

**1. Cuándo empieza y cuándo acaba el año escolar en Irlanda**
`PAA` + `Calendario escolar Irlanda 2026` (related). Direct PAA question, currently
answered by competitors. Outline: the three terms · late Aug/Sep start · June finish ·
Christmas, February mid-term, Easter breaks · how it maps onto the Spanish calendar ·
what that means for a student joining in September vs January.

**2. Qué es el Transition Year y por qué encaja con 4º de la ESO**
PAA: *"¿Cómo se llama 4 de la ESO en Irlanda?"* — high-intent, and 4º ESO is the most
common year to go. Outline: what TY is · project/work-experience focus, not exams · why
that suits a Spanish student · why 4º ESO is the low-risk year (does not touch EBAU).

**3. Estudiar 4º de la ESO en Irlanda: la guía para padres**
`Año escolar en Irlanda 4 ESO` (related search). The decision article for the most
common cohort. Outline: age and maturity · academic risk · what the year gives back ·
how the family stays in contact · Antonio's verbatim review.

**4. Cuánto cuesta un año escolar en Irlanda y qué cambia el precio**
`año escolar en irlanda precios` 90. **Needs Danny's sign-off** — see point 1. Outline:
the market range with sources · the six variables that move it (duration, school type,
host family vs boarding, flights, insurance, guardianship) · what is usually excluded ·
why Impulse quotes per case.

**5. Trimestre en Irlanda: a quién le encaja media medida**
`Trimestre en Irlanda` (related) + `curso escolar en irlanda` 210. Outline: one term vs
half year vs full year · what changes at each length · the adaptation curve.

**6. Convalidar el curso irlandés en España: cómo funciona de verdad**
PAA-adjacent, high anxiety, and the page already had to be corrected here. Outline: it is

**7. Vacaciones escolares en Irlanda: el calendario completo**

**8. Cómo se elige la familia de acogida (y qué preguntar antes de decir que sí)**
No head term; pure trust content that no competitor writes honestly. Outline: the

**9. Bachillerato en Irlanda: cuándo tiene sentido y cuándo no**
`estudiar bachillerato en irlanda` ~90. Outline: the EBAU risk · Leaving Certificate ·

## Rules for whoever writes these

- Route through **`seo-blog-writer`**, never `geo-pages`. Blog articles are not service
  pages and that routing has already been corrected once.
- **3+ images each.** The 34 new Ireland photos in `public/images/extranjero/` make this
  possible for the Ireland articles — but consent is scoped to the study-abroad section,
  so check `src/data/EXTRANJERO-PHOTO-CONSENT.md` before using them anywhere else.
- Peninsular Spanish, **tú**.
- Answer the title question in the **first 80 words**, extractably. AI Overviews own the
  top of all three money SERPs.
- Never reword a testimonial — pick a different review.
- No prices, no student counts, no monitor ratios, no CEFR claims for named individuals.
