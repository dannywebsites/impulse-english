## How to use this

- **Start with #1, #4, #12.** Best volume-to-difficulty ratios, all genuinely uncovered.
- **#4 is the hub.** Build the internal-link engine around it and point the exam pieces into it —
  the book's interlinking chapter is explicit that the hub earns the authority.
- **Seasonality is real:** Cambridge and class-search terms spike hard in **September** (e.g.
  `inglés para adultos madrid` runs 30/mo but hit 90 last September). Publish Tier 1 before then.
- Every piece goes through `seo-blog-writer` with the Impulse brand JSON, which now carries a
  populated `voiceSample` (verbatim `sobre-nosotros` copy, tú).
- The **≥50% verbatim-PAA gate is relaxed** per your call on 2026-08-07.
