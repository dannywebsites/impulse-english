## Article 23 — Cómo elegir academia de inglés en septiembre sin equivocarte

- **slug** `elegir-academia-ingles-septiembre` · **category** `Local Madrid` · **img index 4**
- **target** `como elegir academia de ingles` · no measured volume · **AI Overview: NO** ·
  **no PAA box** → **write the FAQ by hand**

**Why:** September runs about **3×** the rest of the year for Impulse. This is the decision month
and this is the decision article.

⚠️ **This is not a ranking.** `mejores-academias-madrid` already exists. Write **the questions a
parent should ask before paying**, never a listicle naming rivals. Do not use `--format listicle`
(see `_CONTRACT.md` §6b).

The questions that actually separate academies, and which we can answer straight because our own
answers are published:

1. Centro **preparador** or centro **examinador** — not the same thing, and it decides whether your
   child sits the exam somewhere they have never been.
2. The **maximum number of pupils per class**, as a number. "Grupos reducidos" is an adjective; 10
   is a fact.
3. The **total first-year cost**: monthly fee **plus** matrícula **plus** book. Not the monthly fee.
4. **Who teaches** and with what qualification (TEFL / CELTA / DELTA). "Nativo" is not a
   qualification.
5. The **real timetable** against the school day.
6. What happens **if a pupil misses a class**.
7. **What the level test measures and who runs it** — a 20-question online quiz measures grammar; a
   person measures whether the child freezes when they speak.

Ours, for comparison, stated plainly and not as a boast: 45 € matrícula, book **máx. 40 €**, 64 /
83 / 87 / 94 € al mes, groups of 7 / 10 / 10 / 8, centro preparador oficial Cambridge and official
Linguaskill centre, timetable L/X 10:00–21:30 · M/J 15:30–21:30 · V 13:30–19:30, and JP runs the
25-minute prueba de nivel himself.

**FAQ: write 5–6 questions by hand** (there is no PAA box). Use the real questions parents ask on
the phone in September — when to enrol, whether mid-course entry is possible, what happens if the
level turns out wrong, whether the book is included.

## Article 26 — Mi hijo suspende inglés en la ESO: qué hacer ahora

- **slug** `hijo-suspende-ingles-eso` · **category** `Kids Secondary` · **img index 7**
- **target** `mi hijo suspende ingles` · no measured volume · **AI Overview: YES**

**Why:** the highest-intent moment a parent of an ESO pupil has. AI Overview is YES, so answer the
"what do I do now" question in the **first 80 words**.

⚠️ **`hijo-no-avanza-ingles` exists and covers *not progressing*.** This is **failing**, with ESO
stakes: recuperación, the September convocatoria, how many subjects you can carry, what the
instituto will and will not do. Real overlap risk — **if it starts to feel like the same article,
write a `FLAG:` line and stop**; the fallback is consolidation, which is fine.

The useful, specific content is the ESO machinery: how recovery works, what a *pendiente* means,
the difference between a child who cannot do the grammar and a child who cannot do the exam
technique, and when refuerzo is the answer versus when the level itself is the problem.

**PAA — verbatim:**
- ¿Qué puedo hacer si mi hijo no quiere aprender inglés?
- ¿Qué hacer cuando suspenden a un niño?
- ¿Cómo se recuperan las asignaturas suspendidas en la ESO?
- ¿Con cuántas asignaturas suspendidas puedes pasar de curso?
- ¿Cuáles son los factores de dificultad más comunes para el aprendizaje del inglés?

⚠️ The first one is `hijo-no-avanza-ingles` / `motivar-hijo-ingles` territory. Two sentences and a
link.
⚠️ On *cuántas asignaturas suspendidas* — this is regulated and it varies. State the general rule
carefully or say that it depends on the centre's own criteria. **Do not invent a number.**

## Article 24 — Refuerzo escolar o academia de inglés: cuál necesita tu hijo

- **slug** `refuerzo-escolar-o-academia-ingles` · **category** `Comparison` · **img index 3**
- **target** `clases de refuerzo de ingles` · no measured volume · **AI Overview: YES**

⚠️ **`academia-vs-profesor-particular` and `clases-particulares-vs-academia` both exist.** Check them
before you start. Your distinction is different and narrower: **refuerzo (catching up on the school
syllabus) vs academia (building the language and certifying it)**. Same child, two different
objectives, two different measures of success.

The honest version of this article says both are sometimes right, and gives the parent a test for
telling which they need: is the problem the *marks* or the *language*? A child with a 4 who can
hold a conversation needs exam technique. A child with a 7 who freezes needs the language.

**PAA — verbatim:**
- ¿Cómo puedo reforzar mi inglés?
- ¿Cuál es el curso más recomendado para aprender inglés?
- ¿Cuáles son las 5 habilidades en inglés?
- ¿Es posible aprender inglés en 2 meses?

⚠️ This PAA box drifts adult and generic. Keep the answers short and parent-facing, and top up with
2–3 questions of your own that a parent would actually ask.

**Link:** to `/blog/hijo-suspende-ingles-eso/` (article 26, above) and
`/blog/extraescolar-ingles-o-academia/` (article 25, below) — the three form a small decision

## Article 25 — Extraescolar del colegio o academia: qué cambia de verdad

- **slug** `extraescolar-ingles-o-academia` · **category** `Comparison` · **img index 2**
- **target** `extraescolar de ingles` · no measured volume · **AI Overview: YES**

⚠️ **`ingles-colegio-suficiente` covers *is school English enough*.** This is the **after-school
comparison**: ratio, the level of the group, and the objective. The three differences that actually
matter:

- **Ratio.** A school extraescolar takes the class that signed up, often 15–25. Ours are 7 infantil
  and 10 primaria, by design.
- **Level of the group.** An extraescolar groups by *school year*. An academy groups by *level* —
  which is why the level test exists and why a 4º de primaria pupil can sit with a 5º if that is
  where their English is.
- **Objective.** Convenience and exposure versus a certificate and a measured level. Both are
  legitimate; they are not the same purchase.

⚠️ **This PAA box is weak and off-topic** — lean on the body, not the FAQ:
- ¿Qué es extraescolar y ejemplos?
- ¿Qué tipos de extraescolares hay?
- ¿Qué actividades divertidas puedo hacer para aprender inglés?

Use the first one at most, and **write the rest of the FAQ by hand** from the questions a parent
comparing the two would ask (can we do both, does the school extraescolar prepare for Cambridge,
what happens if the group is too easy for my child).
