## Article 29 — La prueba de nivel de 25 minutos: qué se evalúa y qué te llevas

- **slug** `prueba-nivel-ingles-25-minutos` · **category** `Niveles de inglés` · **img index 9**
- **target** `prueba de nivel de ingles` · **1.300/mo**, **3.600 en septiembre** ·
  **AI Overview: NO**

**Why:** this is **the conversion asset of the batch**. High volume, no AI Overview to fight, and it
is the page every other article points at. Write it early and write it well.

**What makes it work is specificity — the things a competitor's generic "prueba de nivel gratuita"
page never says:**

- it lasts **25 minutes**, it is **free**, it is **in person**, and **JP** (the Director de
  Estudios) does it himself — not a salesperson
- what actually happens in the 25 minutes, minute by minute if you can
- what you leave with: **your CEFR level** and the **recommended group**
- no intermediate form, no obligation
- for a child, what the parent is told afterwards
- ⚠️ **infantil is the exception:** children in infantil get the **1-hour trial class** instead,
  never the prueba de nivel. Say so explicitly in the article — a parent of a 4-year-old landing
  here needs to be redirected, not booked into the wrong thing.

Group sizes are the natural follow-on (7 / 10 / 10 / 8 / 8 / 1:1) because the output of the test is
a *group recommendation*. Link to `/blog/clases-ingles-grupos-reducidos/` (article 31, W08).

**PAA — verbatim:**
- ¿Cómo puedo calcular mi nivel de inglés?
- ¿Cómo saber si mi nivel de inglés es B1 o B2?
- ¿Cuáles son los niveles de examen de inglés A1, A2, B1 y B2?
- ¿Qué es A1, A2, B1, B2, C1 y C2?

⚠️ The second one is article 15's headline question, below. Two sentences and a link. The fourth is
the hub's. Two sentences and a link.

**CTA:** this article *is* the CTA for everything else, so its own CTA is the booking itself →
