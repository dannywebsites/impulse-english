## ⚠️ The one fact that governs all four

**In Spain there is no official equivalence between a school stage and a CEFR level.** Not for the
ESO, not for Bachillerato. The curriculum sets *aims*; it does not award a certificate. A pupil
leaves 4º ESO having been *taught towards* something in the A2–B1 region and leaves Bachillerato
around B1–B2, but nobody is *given* a B1 by finishing school.

That is the honest answer, it is what the hub article (`/blog/guia-niveles-ingles-a1-c2/`, already
live) states, and **all four of your articles must agree with it.** Do not write "con la ESO tienes
un A2" as a fact. Write what the curriculum aims at, then say plainly that the only way to hold a
level is to certify it.

Where you need a source, the curriculum decrees (BOE / Comunidad de Madrid) and the Council of
Europe CEFR descriptors are the citable ones. Never invent a percentage or a study.
