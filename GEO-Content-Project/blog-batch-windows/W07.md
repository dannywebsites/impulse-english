## Article 39 — Qué certificados de inglés existen en España y cuál te conviene

- **slug** `certificados-ingles-espana` · **category** `Comparison` · **img index 10**
- **target** `certificados ingles` / `certificados de ingles` · **157 impressions combined,
  positions 31–33, zero clicks**
- Straight out of the Search Console gap list — a search where **nothing we own ranks**.

**Why:** the clearest unserved gap in the whole data set. We have 25+ articles about *individual*
exams and **no page that compares the certificate landscape**. This is the natural top-of-funnel
entry for a parent (or adult) who does not yet know Cambridge from Trinity from Linguaskill from
Aptis from the EOI.

**Shape:** a comparison table (HTML `<table>`) across the families — Cambridge, Trinity, Linguaskill,
Aptis, IELTS, TOEFL, EOI — with what each is for, who accepts it, whether it expires, and roughly
what it costs. Then a short "which one you actually need" section organised by *purpose*: school
child, university access, máster, oposiciones, job abroad.

⚠️ **Be neutral about every certificate that is not ours to sell.** We prepare Cambridge and
Linguaskill; that is a fact we state, not a reason to disparage Trinity or Aptis. Neutrality is the
product here, exactly as in article 18.

⚠️ Do not use `--format listicle` — a certificate is not a business and Impulse cannot be "entry 1"
(see `_CONTRACT.md` §6b). Guide with a table.

**No PAA list is supplied for this one** (it came from the GSC gap list, not a SERP pull). The

## Article 18 — Trinity o Cambridge: cuál le conviene a tu hijo

- **slug** `trinity-o-cambridge-hijo` · **category** `Comparison` · **img index 6**
- **target** `trinity o cambridge` · 40/mo, peak 90 sept · **AI Overview: YES**

**Why:** small but genuinely winnable — page 1 is small academies and Reddit, with **no
cambridgeenglish.org**. And GSC shows we already rank **position 2–3 for every Trinity query we
see**. The authority is there.

⚠️ **Be neutral. Trust is the product.** We are a Cambridge preparation centre, so the reader will
assume bias — the way to beat that assumption is to state honestly where Trinity is the better
choice (its speaking-only GESE grades, its flexibility, its lower cost at some levels, the fact that
many Madrid schools run Trinity in-house) and let the comparison stand. A parent who feels sold to
leaves.

Cover: what each one is, the level equivalence, format differences (Trinity's one-to-one speaking
exam vs Cambridge's four papers), where each is recognised, cost, and how each is usually taken
(school vs centre).

**PAA — verbatim:**
- ¿Qué es mejor, el Trinity o el Cambridge?
- ¿Cuál es la equivalencia entre los exámenes Trinity y Cambridge?
- ¿Es Trinity lo mismo que Cambridge?
- ¿Qué título de inglés tiene más prestigio?
- ¿Qué certificado de inglés tiene más validez?
- ¿Qué es más fácil, Cambridge o Trinity?

⚠️ *Más prestigio* / *más validez* / *más fácil* are three invitations to say something unfounded.
Answer each with what is verifiable — who recognises what, and what the format difference means —
not with a verdict we cannot support.

## Article 27 — Inglés para la EBAU: qué se evalúa y cómo se prepara

- **slug** `ingles-ebau-selectividad` · **category** `Kids Secondary` · **img index 7**
- **target** `ingles ebau` · 20/mo · **AI Overview: NO** · **no PAA box** →
  **write the FAQ by hand**

**Why:** a clean gap. Nothing on the blog covers it and the Secundaria page already sells it. Low
volume, high stakes, and the parent searching this is two months from a decision.

**Content:** what the English paper in the Madrid EBAU actually contains, how it is weighted, what
the *ponderación* means for the degree the pupil wants, and where the exam differs from a Cambridge
exam (it is a reading-and-writing paper marked in Spain, not a four-skills certificate) — which is
exactly why a B2 does not automatically produce a high EBAU mark, and vice versa.

⚠️ **The EBAU is regionally set and changes.** Be careful with structure and weighting details:
attribute them to the Comunidad de Madrid / the university district and say which year they apply
to. If you are not confident about a specific weighting, describe the shape and leave the number
out. **Do not invent a mark scheme.**

**FAQ by hand** — 5–6 questions: does a B2 help in the EBAU, what mark is needed, is the English
paper compulsory, how early to start, can you sit both in the same year.

**Link:** `/cursos-ingles/secundaria/`, article 12 `/blog/nivel-ingles-bachillerato-b1/` (W04), and
article 13 `/blog/edad-sacar-b2-ingles/` (W04) — the "should we do the B2 in 2º de bachillerato
too" question is theirs.

## Article 28 — El verano le ha borrado el inglés: por dónde se recupera

- **slug** `repasar-ingles-verano` · **category** `Learning Methods` · **img index 3**
- **target** `repasar ingles en verano` · 10/mo · **AI Overview: YES** ·
  **the brief marks this "publish late August"**

**Why:** the September re-entry moment. Write it now — it is evergreen and late August is days away.

**Angle:** the honest one. Two months without English does not erase what a child knows; it erases
*fluency of access* — the words are there but slow. That distinction tells the parent not to panic
and also why "repasar" (revisiting) works better than restarting. Then: what actually helps in the
last two weeks of August, what a first week back looks like, and when the gap is big enough that
the child is in the wrong group rather than out of practice.

⚠️ **This PAA box is polluted** with off-topic results (a Mexican academy's prices). **Use only the
relevant questions** and write the rest by hand:
- ¿Cómo puedo aprender inglés en verano?
- ¿Aprender inglés en 3 meses es posible?
- ¿Cuánto tiempo tarda una persona normal en aprender inglés?

⚠️ The third one is `cuanto-tiempo-aprender-ingles`'s question and article 32's neighbour
(`/blog/cuanto-tarda-subir-nivel-ingles/`, W05). Two sentences and a link.

⚠️ Existing neighbours to check and link, not duplicate: `quince-minutos-de-ingles-al-dia` shape
