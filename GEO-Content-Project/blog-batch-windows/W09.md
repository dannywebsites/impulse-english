## Article 35 — Retomar el inglés después de años: por dónde se empieza

- **slug** `retomar-ingles-despues-anos` · **category** `Learning Methods` · **img index 9**
- **target** `retomar el ingles` · no measured volume · **AI Overview: YES**

**Why:** the adult who studied English at school, has not used it in fifteen years, and assumes they
are a beginner. They are not.

**The angle: the false beginner is not a beginner.** They have a large passive vocabulary, decent
reading, wrecked retrieval speed, and no confidence — which is a completely different starting point
from A0, and it means the right first move is a level test rather than a beginner course. Being put
in an A1 group is the single most common reason an adult quits again in November.

⚠️ **`por-que-no-avanzo-ingles` is adjacent.** That page is about being stuck while studying; this is
about **restarting after a gap**. Link, do not restate.

⚠️ **The PAA box for this term is translation intent** (*¿cómo se dice retomar en inglés?*) and is
useless. **Write this FAQ entirely by hand.** Do not use those PAA questions. Good ones: am I
starting from zero, how long until I can hold a conversation again, will I be in a class with
teenagers, what if I have forgotten all the grammar, is one-to-one better at first.

**Link:** article 29 `/blog/prueba-nivel-ingles-25-minutos/` (W05) — the level test *is* the answer
to "por dónde se empieza" — plus `/blog/nivel-intermedio-ingles-estancado/` (article 33, W05) and
`/cursos-ingles/adultos/`.

## Article 44 — Aprender inglés por tu cuenta: qué funciona y qué no

- **slug** `aprender-ingles-por-tu-cuenta` · **category** `Learning Methods` · **img index 8**
- **target** `autoestudio ingles` · **45 impressions, position 62**
- Straight out of the Search Console gap list.

**Why:** self-study intent we are invisible for. **This has to be an honest piece** — that is the
whole reason it can rank and convert. What genuinely works alone, and where a group beats it.

**What works alone:** input volume (reading, series, podcasts), vocabulary systems, grammar
reference, Reading and Listening practice, past papers. Real and effective.

**What does not:** Writing without marking, Speaking without a listener, and — the invisible one —
**you cannot self-diagnose the errors you do not know you make.** A fossilised mistake repeated for
two years is harder to remove than one caught in week three.

The conclusion should be a genuine both/and, not a bait-and-switch: self-study is most of the hours,
a group or a 1:1 is where the correction happens. Natural bridge to article 31
(`/blog/clases-ingles-grupos-reducidos/`, W08).

**No PAA list is supplied** (GSC gap list). Use the live PAA the prewrite returns; top up by hand.

## Article 36 — Inglés para oposiciones: qué nivel piden y cómo se acredita

- **slug** `nivel-ingles-oposiciones` · **category** `Career` · **img index 10**
- **target** `nivel de ingles para oposiciones` · 10/mo · **AI Overview: YES**

⚠️ **Two `linguaskill-oposiciones-*` articles already exist** (`linguaskill-oposiciones-validez`,
`linguaskill-oposiciones-merece-la-pena`). This article is **the level question across oposiciones** —
what level is asked for, how it is scored, and which certificates are accepted — **not a Linguaskill
piece**. Link to those two for the Linguaskill-specific answers.

⚠️ **Be extremely careful with facts here.** Oposiciones requirements are set per convocatoria, per
administration, and they change. **Never state "las oposiciones piden un B1"** as a general fact.
The safe and genuinely more useful framing: requirements vary by convocatoria and body, the *bases*
of each convocatoria are the only authority, and what English usually does is **score points as a
mérito** rather than act as a hard requirement. Then explain how to check: read the bases, find the
baremo, see which certificates are listed by name.

If you cannot verify a specific requirement, **leave the number out**. Do not invent a baremo.

**PAA — verbatim:**
- ¿Qué nivel de inglés te piden para opositar?
- ¿Es necesario el B1 para opositar?
- ¿Cuánto puntúa el B2 en oposiciones?
- ¿Qué es A1, A2, B1, B2, C1 y C2?

⚠️ *¿Cuánto puntúa el B2?* — this genuinely varies. Answer with how the baremo works and where to
look, not with a number we cannot source.
⚠️ The last one is the hub's. Two sentences and a link.

**Link:** the two Linguaskill oposiciones articles, `/blog/certificados-ingles-espana/` (article 39,
W07), `/blog/examen-cambridge-b2-guia-padres/` (article 19, W03), `/cursos-ingles/adultos/`.
