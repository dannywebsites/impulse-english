## Article 43 — Para qué sirve de verdad el B2 First

- **slug** `para-que-sirve-b2-first` · **category** `Cambridge B2 First` · **img index 7**
- **target** `para que sirve el b2 first` · **34 impressions, position 22**

**Why:** every one of our **16 B2 articles covers how to pass it**; not one covers **what it is
actually for**. Different intent, and a natural parent question — the one asked right before paying
the exam fee.

⚠️ **Keep it short and link into article 19** (`/blog/examen-cambridge-b2-guia-padres/`, W03 is

**What it is genuinely for**, honestly ranked by how much it matters:

- **university access in Spain** — many degrees require a B1 or B2 to graduate, and holding it early
  removes a late scramble (link `/blog/b1-universidad-espana/`, which W11 is extending tonight with
  the máster requirement)
- **oposiciones and public-sector baremos** — as a mérito that scores points (link article 36,
  `/blog/nivel-ingles-oposiciones/`, W09)
- **work** — the level most job adverts mean when they write "inglés alto"
- **it does not expire** (link `/blog/certificado-cambridge-caduca/`, article 22, W03) — though
  institutions often ask for something recent
- **the honest limit:** it is not a work visa, not a teaching qualification, and it does not by
  itself make you fluent

## Article 41 — C1 Advanced o C2 Proficiency: cuál tiene sentido después del C1

- **slug** `c1-advanced-o-c2-proficiency` · **category** `Cambridge C1 Advanced` · **img index 8**
- **target** `diferencias entre c1 advanced y c2 proficiency` · **31 + 25 impressions, positions
  37–70**

**Why:** we have **12 C1 articles and nothing on C2 at all**. The top of the ladder is missing
entirely, and people are already searching for the comparison. Note that `examenes-cambridge-guia`
also picks up this exact query at position 70.2 — **this article is the intended owner**, and W13 is
flagging that page rather than fixing it.

**The real question behind the search** is not "what is the difference" but **"is the C2 worth
doing"** — and the honest answer for most Spanish professionals is *usually not*: almost nothing in
Spain requires a C2, the jump from C1 is much larger than C1→B2 was, and the C1 already covers
university, oposiciones and the great majority of jobs. The cases where it *is* worth it: academic
careers, translation and interpreting, teaching English, and a small set of international roles.

Cover the format differences honestly (same four papers, a much higher bar on range and precision,
Proficiency's Reading and Use of English being the step most candidates underestimate), the
guided-hours gap, and the pass scores.

⚠️ **W12 is adding the C1→C2 timing section to `tiempo-b2-a-c1` tonight** — that page ranks 4.8 for
`cuanto tiempo se tarda en pasar del c1 al c2`, so it owns **the timing question**. You own **the
choice**. Give the timing headline and link.

**Link:** article 20 `/blog/examen-cambridge-c1-advanced/` (W03), `/blog/tiempo-b2-a-c1/`,
`/blog/vale-pena-c1-espana/`, `/blog/es-dificil-c1-advanced/`, `/blog/horas-estudio-c1-advanced/`.

## Article 42 — Exámenes Cambridge en Madrid: centros, fechas y cómo elegir

- **slug** `examenes-cambridge-madrid-centros` · **category** `Local Madrid` · **img index 6**
- **target** `examenes cambridge madrid` · **31 impressions, position 29** (plus
  `preparar cambridge madrid` 34 imp @ 28)

**Why:** local + exam intent, which is exactly Impulse's authority as an **official Cambridge
preparation centre** — and we rank nowhere for it.

⚠️ **The distinction this whole article rests on, and the thing to get right:** a **centro preparador**
teaches the exam; a **centro examinador** administers it. Impulse is a **preparation centre**
(accredited by Cambridge English to December 2026) and an **official Linguaskill centre** — so the
Linguaskill is prepared *and sat* here, while a Cambridge exam is prepared here and sat at an
authorised exam centre. State that plainly. Getting it wrong would be a false claim about our own
accreditation, and it is the single easiest thing to get wrong in this article.

⚠️ **Do not use `--format listicle`.** A ranked list of Madrid exam centres would put Impulse at
entry 1 as an exam centre, which is untrue (`_CONTRACT.md` §6b). Write it as a guide with a table.

⚠️ **Do not name and rank competing academies.** Cambridge's own authorised exam centres are
institutions rather than rival academies, and the neutral, factual way to handle them is to point the
reader at **Cambridge's official centre finder** and explain how to read it, rather than publishing a
list that will be stale in a month.

⚠️ **Must not duplicate two other articles:**
- article 3 `/blog/fechas-examenes-cambridge-2026-27/` (**already live**) owns the **calendar**
- article 16 `/blog/inscripcion-examen-cambridge-madrid/` (W02, tonight) owns the **enrolment
  process**

**You own the landscape and the choice:** what kinds of centre exist in Madrid, how preparation and
examination fit together, paper vs computer availability, how to pick a session that suits the school
or work calendar, how far you should be willing to travel on exam day (which matters more than people
think for a twelve-year-old), and what to check before you book.

If you cannot keep clear of 3 and 16, write a `FLAG:` line rather than shipping an overlap.

**Link:** article 3, article 16, `/examenes-cambridge/`, `/examenes-cambridge/fechas-precios/`,
`/blog/que-examen-cambridge-segun-edad/` (article 10, W02).
