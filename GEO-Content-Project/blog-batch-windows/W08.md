## Article 31 — Por qué un grupo de 8 cambia el resultado

- **slug** `clases-ingles-grupos-reducidos` · **category** `Learning Methods` · **img index 10**
- **target** `clases de ingles grupos reducidos` · no measured volume · **AI Overview: NO**

**Why:** **the differentiator nobody local publishes.** Every academy in Madrid says "grupos
reducidos". None of them says the number, and none of them does the arithmetic.

**The arithmetic is the article.** Speaking minutes per student, per hour, by group size. In a
60-minute class where roughly 30 minutes is student talking time: a group of 8 gives each student
about 3,75 minutes; a group of 15 gives 2; a group of 25 gives 1,2. Over a 30-week course that is
the difference between roughly 112 minutes of speaking and 36. Show the working — a reader can check
arithmetic, which is what makes it persuasive rather than promotional.

Be honest about the trade-offs too: a smaller group costs more per head, and a very small group can
lose the variety of voices that makes listening practice real. That is why the adult cap is **8**
and not 4.

**Our exact figures, never a range:** 7 infantil · 10 primaria · 10 secundaria · **8 adultos** ·
8 online · 1:1 particulares.

**PAA — verbatim:**
- ¿Qué es el estilo de enseñanza de grupos reducidos?
- ¿Qué son los grupos reducidos?
- ¿Cuál es el curso más recomendado para aprender inglés?

Only three, so **top the FAQ up by hand** to 5–6 with the questions an adult asks: what happens if
the group is a level above me, can I change groups, what if only three people enrol.

**Link:** into article 29 `/blog/prueba-nivel-ingles-25-minutos/` (W05) — the level test is what
puts you in the right group — and article 44 `/blog/aprender-ingles-por-tu-cuenta/` (W09), which
ends by pointing back here.

## Article 30 — Cómo elegir academia de inglés para adultos: 7 preguntas antes de pagar

- **slug** `elegir-academia-ingles-adultos` · **category** `Local Madrid` · **img index 8**
- **target** `academia de ingles para adultos` · **210/mo**, **720 en septiembre** ·
  **CPC 4,19 €** · **AI Overview: YES**

**Why:** the adult twin of article 23 (W06 is writing the parent version tonight). High commercial
value, and September triples it.

⚠️ **Questions, not a rival ranking.** `mejores-academias-madrid` exists. Do not name or link a
competing academy, and do not use `--format listicle` (`_CONTRACT.md` §6b).

**The seven questions, adult version** — these differ from the parent version because the adult's
constraints are time and evidence, not the school calendar:

1. The **maximum per group**, as a number.
2. The **total first-year cost**: 94 €/mes + 45 € matrícula + libro máx. 40 €. Ask for all three.
3. **What happens when you travel for work** — the missed-class policy is the adult dealbreaker.
4. **Who teaches, and what qualification** (TEFL / CELTA / DELTA).
5. **How your level is set**, and whether a person hears you speak.
6. **How much you will actually talk** — link to article 31.
7. Whether the centre can **certify** you (Cambridge preparation, official Linguaskill centre) or
   only teach you.

Real timetable for the adult reader: **L/X 10:00–21:30 · M/J 15:30–21:30 · V 13:30–19:30.**

**PAA — verbatim:**
- ¿Cuánto tiempo tarda una persona adulta en aprender inglés?
- ¿Cuál es la mejor forma de aprender inglés para un adulto?
- ¿Cuál es el curso más efectivo para aprender inglés?
- ¿Es posible hablar inglés en 3 meses?

⚠️ *¿Cuánto tiempo tarda…?* is article 32's territory (`/blog/cuanto-tarda-subir-nivel-ingles/`,
W05). Headline figure and a link.
⚠️ *¿Es posible hablar inglés en 3 meses?* — answer it honestly. No.

## Article 34 — Cuánto cuesta de verdad aprender inglés de adulto en Madrid

- **slug** `cuanto-cuesta-aprender-ingles-adulto` · **category** `Price` · **img index 8**
- **target** `cuanto cuesta aprender ingles` · 10/mo · **AI Overview: YES**

⚠️ **`precio-clases-ingles-madrid-2026` exists.** This article must be **the total first-year cost for
one adult**, worked through as a single number — or skip it. That is the boundary: the existing page
lists prices, this one adds up a year.

**The sum, ours, stated openly:** 94 €/mes × the course months + **45 €** matrícula + libro
**máx. 40 €**, plus — if the reader wants a certificate — the Cambridge exam fee, **attributed to the
exam centre and its effective date** (Madrid centres currently split `Hasta 31/07/26` /
`Desde 01/08/26`, so add the "revisar cada año" note). Particulares are 29 €/hora if they want the
comparison.

Then the part that makes it useful rather than a price list: **the costs people forget.** The
enrolment fee they did not budget for, the book, the exam, and the biggest one — a year in the wrong
group, which costs the whole year.

**PAA — verbatim:**
- ¿Dónde es más barato aprender inglés?
- ¿Cuál es el curso más efectivo para aprender inglés?
- ¿Es posible aprender inglés en 2 meses?
- ¿Cuánto tarda una persona normal en aprender inglés?

⚠️ *¿Dónde es más barato?* — answer with the categories (EOI, online platforms, academies,
one-to-one) and their real trade-offs. **Do not name a competing academy** as cheap or expensive.

- **slug** `clases-ingles-por-la-manana` · **category** `Local Madrid` · **img index 9**
- **target** `clases de ingles por la manana` · no measured volume · **AI Overview: YES**

**Why:** true, unusual, and nobody else writes it. We genuinely open at **10:00 on Monday and
Wednesday** — that is the article.

**Real timetable, exact:** **L/X 10:00–21:30 · M/J 15:30–21:30 · V 13:30–19:30.** Morning classes
exist on Monday and Wednesday. Do not imply mornings every day.

**Who they actually work for:** shift workers, parents with school-age children and a free morning,
people on reduced hours, freelancers, retirees, and professionals whose evenings are unreliable. And
who they do not work for — anyone with a fixed 9-to-6. Saying that plainly is what makes the piece
trustworthy.

⚠️ **The PAA box for this term is all "aprender inglés gratis"** — wrong intent entirely.
**Write the whole FAQ by hand.** Do not use those PAA questions. Good hand-written ones: are morning
groups smaller, is the level the same, can I switch to an evening group later, are they cheaper.
