# BUSINESS INFORMATION RESEARCH PROMPT
## Use This With Any AI Deep Research Tool

---

## WHAT THIS IS FOR

Use this prompt with AI research tools (ChatGPT Deep Research, Claude, Gemini Deep Research, Perplexity, etc.) to automatically fill out your Business Information Template by analyzing your website.

**Time saved:** 1-2 hours of manual data entry

---

## HOW TO USE

1. Copy the prompt below
2. Open your preferred AI research tool
3. Paste the prompt
4. Attach or upload the `Business-Information-Template.txt` file
5. Provide your website URL
6. Let the AI research and fill out the template
7. Review the output and fill in any "[NO INFORMATION FOUND]" gaps manually

---

## THE PROMPT

Copy everything below this line:

---

**TASK:** Research the following website and fill out the attached Business Information Template as completely as possible.

**WEBSITE TO RESEARCH:** [PASTE YOUR WEBSITE URL HERE]

**INSTRUCTIONS:**

1. **Thoroughly analyze the website** including:
   - Homepage
   - About/About Us page
   - Service pages
   - Location/Contact pages
   - Team/Staff pages
   - Testimonials/Reviews pages
   - Case studies/Portfolio
   - FAQ page
   - Footer information
   - Schema markup (if visible)
   - Google Business Profile (if linked)

2. **Fill out every field in the template** with information found on the website.

3. **For any field where information is NOT found**, enter exactly: `[NO INFORMATION FOUND]`

4. **Do NOT invent or assume information.** Only include facts explicitly stated on the website.

5. **For reviews/ratings**, check:
   - Google Business Profile
   - Yelp listing
   - Any review widgets on the site
   - Testimonial pages

6. **For team members**, look for:
   - About page bios
   - Team page
   - LinkedIn links
   - Author bios on blog posts

7. **For service areas**, check:
   - Service area pages
   - Location pages
   - Footer service area lists
   - Schema markup

8. **For pricing**, look for:
   - Pricing pages
   - Service pages with rates
   - "Starting at" mentions
   - Promotional banners

9. **Return the completed template** in the same format as the original, with all fields filled in or marked `[NO INFORMATION FOUND]`.

10. **At the end, provide a summary** of:
    - How many fields were filled vs. not found
    - Key information gaps that should be prioritized
    - Any additional information found that might be useful but wasn't in the template

## TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS

### Before Running:
- Make sure your website is publicly accessible
- Have any password-protected pages unlocked
- Check that your Google Business Profile is claimed and updated

### Choose the Right Tool:
- **ChatGPT with browsing:** Good for most websites
- **Claude:** Good for analyzing uploaded documents alongside web research
- **Gemini Deep Research:** Good for comprehensive multi-page analysis
- **Perplexity:** Good for finding external information (reviews, citations)

### After Running:
- Review ALL `[NO INFORMATION FOUND]` fields
- Prioritize filling in: team credentials, testimonial details, pricing, case study numbers
- These gaps are often the most valuable for GEO differentiation

---

## WHAT TO DO WITH GAPS

Fields marked `[NO INFORMATION FOUND]` fall into two categories:

### 1. Information Exists But Isn't on Website
You know this information—add it manually:
- License numbers
- Insurance amounts
- Team member details
- Pricing specifics
- Case study outcomes

### 2. Information Doesn't Exist Yet
You need to gather this:
- Customer testimonials (ask clients)
- Case study details (document next project)
- Team credentials (ask staff)
- Review counts (check platforms)

**Pro tip:** The gaps the AI couldn't find are often the same gaps causing your pages to lack differentiation. Filling them improves both your website AND your GEO content.

## ALTERNATIVE: MULTI-SOURCE RESEARCH

For more complete results, ask the AI to also check:

```
Also research these external sources for additional business information:
- Google Business Profile for [Business Name]
- Yelp listing for [Business Name]
- LinkedIn company page for [Business Name]
- Better Business Bureau listing
- Industry directory listings
- Local chamber of commerce
- State licensing board (for license verification)
```

This can fill gaps like review counts, ratings, and credentials that may not be on your website.

---

## PRIVACY NOTE

The AI will only access publicly available information. It cannot access:
- Password-protected pages
- Private customer databases
- Internal documents
- Analytics data

All information gathered is what any visitor to your website could find.
