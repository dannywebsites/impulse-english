# ANTI-PATTERNS: CONTENT THAT KILLS GEO VISIBILITY
## Reference File for GEO Content Project

---

## PURPOSE OF THIS FILE

Before delivering ANY content, check it against the patterns in this file. If the content matches any of these anti-patterns, revise it before delivery.

These patterns signal "duplicate content" or "low-quality template" to AI systems, causing pages to be grouped together (with only ONE getting citations) or ignored entirely.

---

## SECTION 1: TITLE TAG ANTI-PATTERNS

### The Template Format (NEVER USE)
```
[Service] in [City] | [Brand]
[City] [Service] | [Brand]
[Brand] - [Service] in [City]
Best [Service] in [City] | [Brand]
#1 [Service] in [City] | [Brand]
Professional [Service] in [City]
[City] [Service] Company | [Brand]
[Service] Services in [City] and Surrounding Areas
Affordable [Service] in [City] | Call Today
```

### Why These Fail
AI systems see these as templates. When you have:
- "Plumbing Services in Boston | ABC Plumbing"
- "Plumbing Services in Cambridge | ABC Plumbing"
- "Plumbing Services in Somerville | ABC Plumbing"

AI groups them as duplicates and cites only ONE.

### What to Use Instead
Each title must have a unique angle:
- "Emergency Plumber in Boston's Back Bay | 24/7 Brownstone Specialists"
- "Cambridge Victorian Home Plumbing | 15-Year Local Expert"
- "Somerville Triple-Decker Plumbing Pros | Same-Day Service"

## SECTION 2: INTRO PARAGRAPH ANTI-PATTERNS

### Generic Openings (NEVER USE)
```
"Welcome to [Brand], your trusted [service] provider in [City]."
"At [Brand], we pride ourselves on providing quality [service] to [City] residents."
"Looking for [service] in [City]? You've come to the right place."
"[Brand] offers professional [service] to homeowners in [City] and surrounding areas."
"We are [City]'s premier [service] company."
"For over [X] years, [Brand] has been serving the [City] community."
"Our experienced team provides top-quality [service] in [City]."
"[Brand] is a full-service [service] company serving [City]."
"Need [service] in [City]? Call the experts at [Brand]."
"Trust [Brand] for all your [service] needs in [City]."
```

### Why These Fail
- Could be used for ANY location (just swap city name)
- No specific information that proves local expertise
- No numbers, credentials, or differentiators
- Reads like marketing copy, not authoritative content

### What to Use Instead
Answer Capsule format with specific local details:
- Specific neighborhood names (not just city)
- Specific building types or local challenges
- Specific numbers (years, price, response time)
- Named team members
- Details that could ONLY apply to this location

## SECTION 3: SERVICE DESCRIPTION ANTI-PATTERNS

### Swappable Content (NEVER USE)
Any paragraph where you can replace the location name and it still makes sense:

```
"We provide comprehensive [service] to residents of [City]. Our experienced team
handles all types of [service] issues, from minor repairs to major installations.
With years of experience serving the [City] area, we understand what local
homeowners need."
```

**The Swap Test:** Replace [City] with any other location. If the paragraph still works, it's too generic.

### Vague Local References (AVOID)
```
"the Boston area"
"greater Boston"
"the local community"
"this region"
"nearby areas"
"surrounding communities"
```

### What to Use Instead
Specific local references that can't be swapped:
- "Back Bay's 1880s brownstones"
- "the copper piping common in Commonwealth Avenue homes"
- "Cambridge triple-deckers with their shared plumbing stacks"
- "the clay soil foundation issues in Arlington"

## SECTION 5: FAQ ANTI-PATTERNS

### Generic Questions (NEVER USE)
```
"What services do you offer?"
"How do I contact you?"
"What are your hours?"
"Do you offer free estimates?"
"Are you licensed and insured?"
"What is your service area?"
"How much do you charge?"
```

### Generic Answers (NEVER USE)
```
"We offer competitive pricing."
"Contact us today to learn more."
"We serve the greater [City] area."
"Our experienced professionals..."
"We pride ourselves on quality service."
"Call for a free quote."
```

### What to Use Instead
Location-specific questions and answers:
- "How much does a plumber cost in Back Bay?" → "$89 service call, repairs typically $150-$450 for brownstone systems"
- "Can you work on historic brownstone plumbing?" → Specific answer about building types in the area
- "How quickly can you get to Beacon Hill?" → "45 minutes, our technician Mike is based in nearby Somerville"

## SECTION 8: SCHEMA ANTI-PATTERNS

### Copy-Paste Schema (NEVER DO)
Using identical schema across pages with only address changed.

**Signals duplicate content when:**
- Same @id across multiple pages
- Same geo coordinates (impossible!)
- Same areaServed for different locations
- Identical FAQ content

### Incomplete Schema (AVOID)
- LocalBusiness without geo coordinates
- LocalBusiness without areaServed
- Service without pricing
- FAQPage with generic questions
- AggregateRating without reviewCount

### What to Use Instead
Completely unique schema per page:
- Unique @id using page URL
- Correct geo coordinates for that location
- Location-specific areaServed neighborhoods
- Location-specific FAQ questions and answers
- Location-specific ratings if tracked separately

---

## SECTION 9: STRUCTURAL ANTI-PATTERNS

### Template Page Structure
If your Back Bay page and Cambridge page have:
- Same section headings
- Same content flow
- Same testimonial placement
- Same CTA placement
- Only the location name changed

**AI will see them as duplicates.**

### What to Use Instead
Vary the structure based on location characteristics:
- Different lead angles based on local needs
- Different featured case studies
- Different team members highlighted
- Different FAQ questions based on local concerns

## THE UNIVERSAL TEST

Before delivering any content, ask:

1. **The Swap Test:** Can I replace the location with another and have it still work? If YES → revise.

2. **The Verification Test:** Could someone fact-check the specific claims in this content? If NO → add specifics.

3. **The Uniqueness Test:** Is there anything in this content that could ONLY describe THIS location? If NO → add local signals.

4. **The Template Test:** Does this follow a pattern I've used for other pages? If YES → differentiate.

If content fails ANY of these tests, it needs revision before delivery.

## REMEMBER

AI systems are designed to detect patterns. The patterns in this file have been identified as signals of:
- Duplicate content
- Template-based pages
- Low-quality, low-effort content
- Non-authoritative sources

Every pattern you avoid increases your chances of AI citation.
Every unique detail you add differentiates your page from competitors.

When in doubt: **More specific always beats more polished.**
