Research: DataForSEO volumes (Spain, `es`) + live Madrid SERP/PAA pulled **2026-08-07**, and a

---

## 1. How to use this

Each article gives you the **target keyword**, its **volume**, whether Google shows an **AI
Overview**, **why** we're writing it, the **verbatim PAA questions** for your FAQ, and any
**warning** about existing articles you must not duplicate.

**PAA** = the *"Otras preguntas de los usuarios"* box on Google. Every question below was
**copied verbatim from the live Madrid SERP on 2026-08-07**. Do not reword them — keep the `¿`
and `?`. They exist because enough people ask that exact question that Google built a box for
it. Use them as **FAQ headings**; aim for at least half your FAQ to be verbatim PAA.

⚠️ Google's **questions are stable; the answers it shows are volatile** and change between
identical searches. Never copy Google's answer text — write ours.

**AI Overview: YES** means Google publishes its own AI summary above the results. Where you see
it:
- Answer the headline question **in the first 80 words, under 300 characters**, as a plain
  factual statement. That is the passage Google lifts when it cites a source.
- Do not open with scene-setting. Answer first, expand after.
- It's the normal case here, not the exception.

## 2. What the data says — the six findings behind this batch

**1 — Our family pages get seen and never clicked.** 186 real family searches, **2,169
impressions, zero clicks** in 28 days. Not one. The pages Google shows sit at positions 20–78 —
pages 3 to 8. *This is why the batch exists.*

**2 — The blog is what actually earns traffic.** 11,038 impressions and 132 clicks in the same
window — about **81% of all clicks the site got**.

**3 — But the blog is almost entirely adult exam content.** All 13 family-oriented articles
combined earn **1,367 impressions — under 1%** of blog impressions.

**4 — We rank for a lot we fail to convert.** **174 searches in the top 20 with zero clicks —
3,899 impressions wasted.** Worst case: the *"caduca"* cluster (does the certificate expire?) —
**68 searches, 884 impressions, 1 click**, at positions 3 to 10. We rank third and get nothing
because the articles never answer the yes/no question in the opening line.

**5 — The biggest keyword in the set is one we're invisible for.** `test examen cambridge`,
**5.400/month**, **zero impressions**. Nothing to cannibalise, no incumbent to protect.

**6 — 3,038 impressions sit on searches where nothing we own ranks** (position 20+). Group 6
below is built from that list.

⚠️ **Seasonality.** July is Impulse's yearly low point; **September runs about 3×**. Everything
in Groups 1 and 2 should be live **before September**.
